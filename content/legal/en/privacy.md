## 1. Introduction

Finco Capital ("we", "us", "our") values the privacy and security of customer information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal data when you use our website and digital financial services.

This policy is an example template provided for demonstration purposes and does not constitute legal advice.

## 2. Information we collect

- **Information you provide** — such as your name, contact details, and identity documents submitted through forms or applications.
- **Usage information** — such as pages visited, device type, and interactions, collected automatically to improve the Services.
- **Transaction information** — details related to the financial products and services you use.

## 3. How we use your information

- To provide, operate, and improve the Services.
- To process applications and deliver financial products.
- To communicate with you about your account, products, and updates.
- To comply with legal, regulatory, and internal policy requirements.

## 4. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it with trusted service providers, regulators, or authorities where required by law, and only to the extent necessary.

## 5. Data security

We protect customer information within the framework of the relevant laws, regulations, and internal policies, using appropriate technical and organizational measures to guard against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

## 6. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information. To exercise these rights, please contact us using the details in the footer of this website.

## 7. Cookies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to enable core functionality and to understand how the site is used. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

## 8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be posted on this page with a revised "last updated" date.

## 9. Contact

If you have any questions about this policy or how your data is handled, please contact us through the details provided in the footer of this website.
